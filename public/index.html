SMALL HABITS. BIG DIFFERENCE.

A playbook for your next yes.

Three simple ways to make your story land.

01

Give your story a shape.

Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Set the scene briefly, explain your responsibility, spend most of your answer on what you did, and finish with the outcome.

Try this: Take one project from your resume. Write one sentence for each part, then tell it out loud without reading.
02

Think out loud, clearly.

For technical questions, clarify the constraints, explain your approach, compare alternatives, and discuss how you would test it. Make your reasoning easy to follow.

Try this: Explain a design decision to someone who hasn't seen your code. Include one tradeoff and one failure case.
03

Let your words breathe.

A short pause gives you time to think and your listener time to follow. Use a deliberate pause instead of a filler. Say “I” when describing your own contribution.

Try this: Record a 90-second introduction. Listen once for structure and once for pace. Pick one thing to improve.
