# Interview assets

`interviewer-head.glb`, `interviewer-skin.jpg` and `interviewer-normal.jpg` derive from **Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith**, obtained from the [three.js LeePerrySmith example](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith). Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Based on work at www.triplegangers.com. The app trims the scan's shoulder geometry and adds original clothing/hair, lighting, head motion and jaw deformation. The creator does not endorse RU Employed.

The original [license notice](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/LeePerrySmith_License.txt) is retained in this folder.

`face_landmarker.task` comes from Google's [official MediaPipe model distribution](https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task), as documented in the [Face Landmarker guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker). Frames are processed locally and discarded. The MediaPipe runtime is distributed under Apache-2.0; three.js uses MIT. Dependency licenses are included in their installed packages.
